About Department

The Department of English at Sri Venkateswara College encourages students to enjoy reading, think creatively, and express their ideas with confidence. Our classes include literature from different times and places.

Students learn through classroom discussions, presentations, reading groups and department events.

Courses Offered

Students can explore literature and language through these courses:

  • B.A. (Honours) English
  • British Poetry and Drama
  • Indian Writing in English
  • Literary Criticism and Theory
  • Language, Literature and Culture

Academic Activities

Our department holds activities where students can learn, share their work and meet other readers.

Seminars

Students and guest speakers discuss books, writers and ideas.

Workshops

Creative writing and theatre workshops help students try new skills.

Events

Book readings, poetry sessions and the annual literary day bring the department together.
